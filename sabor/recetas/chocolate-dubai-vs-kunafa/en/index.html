Method

1

Cut the kataifi into 1/2-inch pieces.

2

Melt the butter in a skillet over medium heat, add the kataifi and stir nonstop for 5 to 7 minutes, until golden and crispy. Don't walk away: it burns in seconds. Let it cool completely.

3

Mix the cooled kataifi with the pistachio cream, tahini and salt.

4

Melt the chocolate in a double boiler or in the microwave in 30-second bursts, stirring each time. Take it off when a few pieces are still unmelted and stir until they melt.

5

Brush the mold with a layer of chocolate, sides too. Freeze 10 minutes.

6

Fill with the pistachio mixture, pressing well and leaving a few millimeters free at the top.

7

Cover with the rest of the chocolate and smooth it. Refrigerate 1 hour, unmold and cut in half.

THE TRICK

Don't overheat the chocolate: if it gets too hot it turns dull and grainy. Take it off while a few pieces are still unmelted and finish melting it by stirring.
