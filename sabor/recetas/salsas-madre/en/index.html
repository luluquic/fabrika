Homemade Béchamel (and its daughter, Mornay sauce)

IngredientsMethodStory and video
ServesAbout 2 cups of sauce

Ingredients

Béchamel
For Mornay (optional)
Thicker, for croquettes

Method

1

Warm the milk without letting it boil.

2

In a heavy-bottomed pot over medium-low heat, melt the butter and add all the flour at once.

3

Cook the roux 1 or 2 minutes, stirring, until it smells like crackers, without browning.

4

Add the milk little by little (just a splash at first), whisking nonstop with a balloon whisk.

5

With all the milk in, keep whisking and let it simmer gently 8 to 10 minutes, until it coats the back of a spoon.

6

Season at the end with salt, pepper and nutmeg. If there are lumps, strain it.

7

For Mornay: turn off the heat, add the grated cheese and stir until it melts.

8

To store it, press plastic wrap onto the surface so it doesn't form a skin.

THE TRICK

1) Cook the roux 1 to 2 minutes without browning: the raw-flour taste goes away and the sauce stays white. 2) Add the milk little by little, whisking nonstop: that's how you avoid lumps.
