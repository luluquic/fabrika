Method

1

Set aside a handful of corn kernels.

2

Blend the rest of the corn with the milk, egg, sugar, salt, melted butter and corn meal into a thick, pancake-like batter.

3

Add the reserved kernels and pulse the blender just 2 or 3 times.

4

Heat a budare or heavy skillet over medium-low heat and butter it.

5

Pour a ladle of batter (about 5 in / 12 cm). Don't touch it.

6

When the edges look dry and it smells like toasted sugar (3 to 4 minutes), flip it with a wide spatula and cook 2 to 3 more minutes.

7

Lay thick slices of cheese on the hot cachapa, fold it in half and wait a minute.

8

Serve with butter on top and, if you like, chicharrón on the side.

THE TRICK

1) Save a handful of kernels and add them at the end with 2 or 3 pulses: that's the homemade texture. 2) Medium-low heat, and don't flip until the edges are dry and it smells like toasted sugar; flip it early and it breaks.
