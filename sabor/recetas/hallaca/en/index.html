Hallacas caseras (versión fácil)

IngredientsMethodStory and video
ServesAbout 12 hallacas

Ingredients

Stew (make it the day before)
Annatto oil
Dough
Garnish and assembly

Method

1

Sauté the onion, bell pepper, garlic, scallions and sweet peppers.

2

Add the meats, wine, papelón, salt, pepper and cumin. Simmer on low for 1 1/2 hours. Refrigerate the stew until the next day and save the broth.

3

Heat the oil with the annatto seeds and strain it.

4

Mix the corn meal with the warm broth, half of the annatto oil and salt. Knead until smooth.

5

Wipe the leaves with a damp cloth, cut them and pass them through hot water so they don't tear.

6

Brush each leaf with annatto oil, add a ball of dough and press it very thin.

7

Add 3 tablespoons of stew and the garnish: an olive, a few raisins, a few capers and a pepper strip.

8

Fold the leaf, wrap it in a second leaf and tie it crosswise with twine.

9

Boil the hallacas in salted water for 1 hour. Let them rest a few minutes before opening.

THE TRICK

1) Make the stew the day before and refrigerate it: it firms up and filling is much easier. 2) Brush the leaf with annatto oil before the dough: it doesn't stick and the hallaca comes out glossy.

The story

Everyone repeats that the hallaca was born from leftovers masters gave to enslaved people. What do the sources say? What is documented: "corn hallacas" in 1538, a woman selling hallacas from her home in 1756, a written recipe in 1899, and how it only became a Christmas dish in the 20th century. We separate history from legend. Then easy homemade hallacas, with 2 tips.
