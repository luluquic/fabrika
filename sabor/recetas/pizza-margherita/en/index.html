Method

1

Mix the flour, water, salt and yeast. Knead 10 minutes until smooth, cover and let it rest.

2

Keep the dough in the fridge 24 to 48 hours. Take it out 2 hours before and divide it into two balls.

3

Crush the peeled tomatoes with salt and a splash of olive oil. Don't cook it.

4

Put the pizza stone on the top rack, turn the oven to its highest setting and preheat 45 minutes.

5

Stretch each ball with your fingers from the center out, no rolling pin, leaving a puffy edge.

6

Spread about 3 tablespoons of sauce and bake about 5 minutes.

7

Add about 3.5 oz (100 g) of torn mozzarella and bake 2 or 3 more minutes.

8

Out of the oven, finish with fresh basil leaves and a drizzle of olive oil.

THE TRICK

1) Leave the dough 24 to 48 hours in the fridge: it tastes better and stretches easily. 2) Preheat the pizza stone 45 minutes on the top rack, at the highest setting.

The story

In a Naples pizzeria hangs a letter with a royal seal from 1889: it says Queen Margherita tried three pizzas and found them delicious. We test 3 myths: that it was invented for the queen, that the letter proves it, and that it was built with the colors of the flag. What historian Zachary Nowak found (the seal, the signature and a surname that wasn't the pizza maker's), why the EU's own regulation dates the Margherita to 1796–1810, and what happened in 1932. Then how to make a Margherita pizza in a home oven, with 2 tips.
