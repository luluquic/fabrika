Fabrika

A private video production studio for history and economics storytelling.

What Fabrika does

Fabrika is a desktop tool used only by its owner, Luis Carlos Delgado. It turns written scripts into finished videos (narration, images, maps, animations and short clips) and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channels.

  • Produces long-form videos and Shorts from the owner's scripts.
  • Uploads finished videos, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and captions to the owner's channels through the official YouTube Data API.
  • Uploads are saved as private so the owner can review and schedule them in YouTube Studio.

Channels

  • Guerra y Capital — military history seen through money, finance and the world economy (Spanish).

Who can use it

Fabrika is not a public service. It has no user accounts and is used only by its owner to manage channels he owns.

Qué es Fabrika (español)
