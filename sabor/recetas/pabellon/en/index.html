Pabellón criollo

IngredientsMethodStory and video
ServesServes 4

Ingredients

Shredded beef
Black beans
Rice
Fried sweet plantains

Method

1

Cook the flank steak in salted water until very tender: 1 1/2 hours (or 40 minutes in a pressure cooker). Save the broth.

2

Let the meat cool and shred it with your hands or two forks.

3

Sauté the chopped onion, bell pepper and garlic; add the tomato and cumin, then the meat with 1 cup of broth, and cook 15 minutes.

4

Cook the soaked beans in water until tender (about 1 1/2 hours). At the end, add a sauté of onion, bell pepper and garlic, the cumin and salt (and the sugar, if you like).

5

Make the rice with 4 cups of the beef broth, the oil and salt.

6

Cut the plantains on a diagonal and fry them over medium heat, slowly, until golden and caramelized.

7

Serve the rice, beans and beef side by side, with the plantains next to them or around (con barandas). With a fried egg on top it's pabellón a caballo.

THE TRICK

The plantain peel must be almost black; if it's yellow, it won't caramelize. And don't throw out the beef broth: cook the rice in it and the whole plate tastes like pabellón.
