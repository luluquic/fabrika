Ceviche peruano clásico

ServesServes 4

Ingredients

Ceviche
On the side

Method

1

Cook the sweet potato and corn and let them cool.

2

Cut the fish into 3/4-inch (2 cm) cubes and keep it in the fridge.

3

Slice the onion very thin and soak it in ice water.

4

Squeeze the limes without pressing too hard (the peel turns it bitter).

5

In a cold glass bowl, mix the fish with salt and the chile.

6

Add the lime juice, drained onion and cilantro. Toss gently and wait 2 or 3 minutes, no more.

7

Taste the leche de tigre (the liquid at the bottom), adjust the salt and serve right away with the sweet potato and corn.

THE TRICK

Everything must be cold: the fish, the bowl and the limes. That keeps it fresh and the fish doesn't turn tough.
