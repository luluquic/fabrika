Dominican Mangú with the Three Hits (Los Tres Golpes)

ServesServes 4

Ingredients

Los tres golpes
Equipment

Method

1

Peel the plantains: cut off the ends, slit them lengthwise and pull off the peel with your finger.

2

Cut them into chunks and boil them in salted water for 15 to 20 minutes, until a fork slides in easily.

3

Meanwhile, sauté the onion rings in the oil for a couple of minutes with the vinegar and salt, until soft and pink.

4

Take the plantain out of the water and mash it right away, while still hot.

5

Add the butter and keep mashing, pouring in the cup of cold water little by little until smooth and creamy.

6

Fry the salami and cheese slices until golden, and fry one egg per person.

7

Serve the mangú with the onion on top and los tres golpes on the side.

THE TRICK

1) Mash the plantain as soon as it comes out of the water: if it cools, it turns stiff and lumpy. 2) Pour the cold water little by little while mashing: that keeps it creamy, not gluey.

The story

Why is mangú called mangú? Everyone says a U.S. soldier tasted the plantain mash in 1916 and said "man, good." But the word was already in the Listín Diario newspaper in 1899, seventeen years earlier.
