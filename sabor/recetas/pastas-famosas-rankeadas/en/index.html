Homemade Genoese Pesto

Method

1

Wash the basil and dry it very well with a clean towel.

2

In the mortar, crush the garlic with the coarse salt into a paste; add the pine nuts and crush.

3

Add the basil a little at a time and grind it by rotating against the sides of the mortar, without pounding. (With a blender: chill the jar 10 minutes in the freezer and blend in short pulses.)

4

Stir in the parmesan and pecorino, and finally drizzle in the oil, mixing with a spoon.

5

Cook the pasta in plenty of salted water and save half a cup of the cooking water.

6

In a bowl, off the heat, toss the pasta with the pesto and a splash of the water until creamy. Serve right away.

THE TRICK

1) Bone-dry basil and no heat: in a blender, use a cold jar and short pulses. 2) Never put pesto in a hot pan: toss it off the heat with a splash of pasta water.
