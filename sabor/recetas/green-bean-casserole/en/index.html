Method

1

Boil the green beans 5 minutes in salted water and move them to cold water. (If canned, just drain them.)

2

Mix the mushroom soup, milk, soy sauce, chipotle, adobo sauce and garlic. Start with 1 chipotle. Add the cheese if you like.

3

Mix the green beans with the sauce and 2/3 cup of fried onions.

4

Move it to the baking dish and bake at 350 °F (175 °C) for 25 minutes.

5

Take out the dish, stir and top with the other 2/3 cup of fried onions.

6

Bake 5 more minutes, until the onions are golden.

7

Let it rest 5 minutes and serve.

THE TRICK

The onions on top go in for the last 5 minutes, not from the start: that keeps them crispy instead of burnt.

The story

No grandmother invented green bean casserole. Dorcas Reilly created it in 1955, in Campbell's test kitchen in Camden, New Jersey, for an Associated Press food feature: it had to use pantry staples… and the company's cream of mushroom soup. I'll tell you why that 1934 can was both a soup and a sauce, which ingredients didn't make the cut (ham, celery salt, Worcestershire), why it has soy sauce, what it was first called ("Green Bean Bake") and how it reached Thanksgiving once the recipe went on the can's label.
