Method

1

Peel the potatoes and cut them into sticks 3/8 inch (1 cm) thick.

2

Rinse them in cold water until it runs clear, soak 30 minutes and dry them very well with a towel.

3

Heat the oil to 320 °F (160 °C) in a deep pot, filling it no more than halfway.

4

Fry the potatoes in batches for 5 to 6 minutes, until soft but still pale.

5

Take them out and let them rest at least 20 minutes.

6

Raise the oil to 350 °F (180 °C) and fry them again 2 or 3 minutes, until golden and crispy.

7

Drain, salt right away and serve with mayonnaise. Never leave hot oil unattended.

THE TRICK

1) Remove the starch: rinse, soak 30 minutes and dry very well. 2) Double fry: 320 °F (160 °C) to cook the inside, a 20-minute rest, and 350 °F (180 °C) to brown them. Use a thermometer instead of guessing.
